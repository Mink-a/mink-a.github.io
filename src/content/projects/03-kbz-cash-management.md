## Overview

A full-stack internal tool for KBZ Bank's cash operations team. Reads from branch and ATM cash positions, suggests redistribution plans, and tracks the resulting cash-in-transit operations through to settlement.

## My role

Full-stack engineering across the React frontend and the NestJS API service. Worked with Oracle DB for transactional integrity and historical cash-position reporting.
