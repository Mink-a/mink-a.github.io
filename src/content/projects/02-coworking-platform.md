## Overview

A platform for coworking-space operators to manage members, desks, meeting rooms, and access — with a member-facing app for booking and billing and an operator dashboard for the day-to-day.

## What I built

- Booking engine: desk and room availability, recurring bookings, cancellation windows.
- Member directory + check-in flow tied to the shared face-identity service.
- Operator dashboard: occupancy, utilization, and revenue reporting.
