## Overview

I built CoBudget for the situation that started it: a trip with friends where everyone fronts different expenses and nobody wants to do spreadsheet math at the end. A small PWA, install-anywhere, no account required to use.

## Features

- Add expenses to a shared group with custom split ratios.
- Live running totals of who owes who.
- Offline-first — works on weak connections, syncs when online.
