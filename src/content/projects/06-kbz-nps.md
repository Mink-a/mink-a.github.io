## Overview

An internal analytics platform at KBZ Bank for collecting and analyzing Net Promoter Score data. Captures responses from multiple channels, computes NPS by segment, and visualizes trends so product owners can act on the data.

## My role

Full-stack engineering across the React dashboard and the NestJS API. Oracle DB for the data warehouse layer.
