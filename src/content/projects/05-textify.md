## Overview

Textify turns photos and scans of physical documents into searchable, structured text. Built to scratch a real itch — old paper records that I wanted indexed and grep-able.

## Features

- Upload from camera, file, or paste.
- OCR pipeline with cleanup pass for common errors.
- Export to plain text, Markdown, or JSON.
