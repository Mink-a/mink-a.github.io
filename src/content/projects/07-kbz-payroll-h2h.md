## Overview

A frontend application for KBZ Bank's corporate payroll customers. Uploads payroll files, validates them against bank rules in real time, and tracks disbursement status across the host-to-host pipeline.

## My role

Frontend engineering — React with Redux for the multi-step upload/validation/disbursement state machine, and TypeScript throughout for type safety against the payroll schema.
