Pyidaungsu 2.5.3 — Myanmar Unicode font
Copyright (c) Myanmar Computer Federation (MCF) / Myanmar Unicode & NLP Research Center.

Pyidaungsu is the official Myanmar Unicode font, distributed freely for public use.

The files served here are subsets of Pyidaungsu 2.5.3 Regular and Bold, reduced to
the Myanmar Unicode blocks and converted to woff2:
  - pyidaungsu-myanmar.woff2       (Regular)
  - pyidaungsu-myanmar-bold.woff2  (Bold)

Source: https://mcf.org.mm/pyidaungsu-font  (downloads via mmunicode.org.mm)
