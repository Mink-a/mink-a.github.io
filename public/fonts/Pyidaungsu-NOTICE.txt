Pyidaungsu — Myanmar Unicode font
Copyright (c) 2016 Myanmar Computer Federation (MCF).

Pyidaungsu is the official Myanmar Unicode font, distributed freely for public use.

The file served here (pyidaungsu-myanmar.woff2) is a subset of Pyidaungsu Regular,
reduced to the Myanmar Unicode blocks and converted to woff2.

Source: https://github.com/saturngod/myanmar-unicode-fonts
